About DIVA

The collective voice of imported vehicle dealers.

DIVA stands for Dealers in Imported Motor Vehicles Association. Public records and press coverage identify DIVA as the association representing operators in Mauritius dealing principally with imported second-hand and reconditioned motor vehicles.

DIVA engages with public authorities on matters affecting the sector, including vehicle import rules, taxation, age limits, hybrid and electric vehicle policy, inspection requirements, consumer protection, dealer obligations and the wider competitiveness of the automotive market.
